# Cat collection — v6.6.0

Mochi is always present. The collection contains **99 collectible friends plus Mochi = 100 cats total**. At most three friends can visit the room simultaneously; the collection gallery is paginated 12 at a time so a large collection does not burden the tablet renderer.

## Cat Points

Cat unlocks use a single transparent Cat Point total:

- **+1 point** for each already-validated planner learning milestone. This preserves the original friend unlock logic.
- **+2 points** once when a non-rest Maths subject reaches 2× its planned foreground minutes for that local day.
- **+2 points** once when a non-rest Science subject reaches 2× its planned foreground minutes for that local day.

The normal daily quest still ends at 1×. The ordinary study clock still stops there. After the planned day is complete, optional bonus activity can restart that subject clock toward 2×. The bonus clock stops at 2×; there is **no time reward beyond 2×**. Thus time can contribute at most four Cat Points per day when both scheduled subjects are doubled. Rest-day subjects cannot earn the bonus.

Foreground time remains an estimate, not a learning score. Cat Points do not change mastery, question selection, paper evidence or readiness assessments. They are a collection reward only.

## Unlock curve and compatibility

Miso, Suki, Kumo and Yuki retain their original thresholds of 2, 8, 18 and 32 points. Later thresholds rise gradually toward a long-term final unlock above 900 Cat Points. Existing unlocked cats never relock if older history is trimmed. Existing version-1 rosters migrate to the new version-2 collection record; deliberate visitor choices are preserved.

The double-time ledger stores only one key per subject/day and merges by union across family devices. Repeated saves, clock restarts and time beyond 2× cannot create another award for the same subject/day.
