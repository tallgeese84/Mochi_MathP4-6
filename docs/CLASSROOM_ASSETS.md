# Classroom illustration provenance

Six original raster illustrations were generated for this course on 22 September 2026 using the image-generation tool. They were visually inspected and converted from PNG to WebP at up to 1200 × 800 pixels, quality 82, without changing their depicted content. The WebP files in `course/assets/` are the published assets and are included in the release-scoped offline cache.

| Asset | Art direction and teaching use |
|---|---|
| maths-workbench.webp | Cream and purple painted maths materials: fraction pieces, bars, blocks and notebook. A first version included an invalid five-square net; it was rejected and the net replaced with a plain notebook through image editing. Exact nets and quantities are taught separately. |
| living-world.webp | Detailed original pond, plants, fungi and animals in gouache style. Used as observational context; the picture does not establish species, diet or a measured community. |
| water-landscape.webp | Original painted mountains, water, clouds and rain, with cold-water objects in the foreground. Used to connect changes of state to environmental contexts. It is not a measured map or process diagram. |
| inquiry-workbench.webp | Original painted plant comparison with a shade box and different starting plant sizes. Presented explicitly as a proposed setup to critique; never treated as experimental results. |
| plant-study.webp | Close original botanical study of young bean plants, visible roots, soil and observation tools, in gouache and pencil with lavender shadows. No labels or arrows. Used to observe parts and ask questions; not an exact anatomical reference. |
| science-bench.webp | Separate magnet, clips, wooden block, water, paper and observation tools on a cream and purple bench. No functioning electrical setup or claimed measurements. Used for materials and interactions context. |

Alt text and captions are in `course-ui.js`. Illustrations do not replace a scientific explanation, establish quantities or reveal answers in independent checks. Exact geometry, relationships and flows are rendered by `course-visuals.js`; numerical models have stated assumptions. Figures and labels occupy separate layout space. The cube net has six distinct face directions when folded, verified independently in tests.

No emoji artwork, stock photo or copied textbook illustration was added to the classroom. Existing app artwork remains part of the original question studio.
