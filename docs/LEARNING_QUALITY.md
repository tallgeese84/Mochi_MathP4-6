# v7.6.0 — reliable records and focused learning

## Question identity, not screen position
Both subject editors bind captures to the rendered practice ID or paper ID/index.
A new draft cannot receive answer text, notes or handwriting from the previous screen.
Mixed-practice, manual practice, second-look and paper navigation use the same guard.
Returning to the same question still restores its saved fields. The mixed-question
number now refers to the question on screen, including while its feedback is open.
Old records are not erased or guessed at: historical carry-over cannot be reliably
reconstructed. Use clean fresh checks when interpreting earlier doubtful work.

## Written science: feedback is provisional
New written responses carry gradingVersion 2, separate from the question revision.
The local checklist now requires contextual repetition/replication: an unrelated
use of “mean” is not an averaging step. Negated repetition is not credited. An
explicit claim that hot water melts sugar is flagged for discussion; saying sugar
does not melt is not flagged. These rules are limited heuristics, not semantic
assessment of all scientific language. Spelling-tolerant legacy key-idea checks
remain preliminary; alternative valid wording may require adult review.

Written responses do not automatically become independent mastery. A grown-up
must read the actual submitted explanation and record a valid review of that
response. The review timestamp must not precede its last submission. A revision,
help, a reveal or a conflict remains disqualifying for first-attempt independence.
A valid adult review can recognise wording the checklist misses, without changing
the historical automatic mark. Pending review is not treated as a diagnosed
conceptual error or an endless retry/redo requirement. The student can revise,
recheck, or save for review and continue without revealing the model answer.

Historical response marks retain version 1. Existing paper questions, blueprints,
marks and deadlines are frozen; old automatic written paper subtotals remain
provisional, not verified explanation scores. The parent review panel explicitly
shows this distinction and the current corrected checklist. Review records sync
with existing backup/merge logic; no private provider credentials are required.

## Smaller geometry steps
Two new micro-bridge units sit before complex surface-area work:
1. Find the area of one face: identify the requested rectangle and its two edges;
   distinguish square units from the third dimension inside the box.
2. Pair matching faces: one face, its opposite matching face, then three pairs
   for the complete shell. Inverse questions recover a missing edge from face area.

These include conceptual checks, worked solutions, bounded numerical variants,
static face diagrams and the existing optional 2D/3D teaching tools. They lead into
the existing six-face and hidden-join lesson. New complex-solid mixed questions
respect missing prerequisites. Existing advanced independent evidence is not reset.
The short algebra-strength interlude and delayed review remain. Each new lesson
can earn one existing-style exposure Cat Point, not a mastery badge or repeat reward.

## Less repetitive science
Revision 3 practice adds six circuit states, in diagram and connection-table
representations: series/parallel, open common connection, one open branch, or all
connections closed. Explanation questions ask the learner to trace a full return
route through the battery, rather than recognise a familiar answer position.
Dissolving/melting work asks about mass and evidence of solute recovery. Repetition
questions distinguish comparisons across temperatures from repeated trials at
each temperature. Teaching additions are inside the existing relevant lessons.

After three completed same-topic questions on the local day, normal/manual
practice offers a different task. With enough application evidence, circuits
first offers a written transfer case. While that explanation awaits review,
practice uses another topic instead of repeatedly assigning the same explanation.
Active papers, unfinished drafts, scheduled recalls and saved mixed/redo work keep
their priorities. Explicit guided revisits remain possible. A familiar question
is never fresh science mastery merely because its finite template pool is exhausted.
The limit is a design choice, not a psychometrically validated learning threshold.

## Preservation and validation
No learner records or copied school-report text are included in the repository.
The homepage, time goals, tutor connection, prior cats, coins and wardrobes remain.
Old science questions use their recorded revision; new mixed queues use revision 3.
Existing historical mock papers stay at revision 2, original papers at revision 1.

Tests cover contextual grading, negation, reference answers, human review, stale
reviews, assistance/revision flags, historical marking and cloud merge, finite
question diversity, repetition routing, prerequisite gating and bounded rewards.
Independent quantitative checks count unit squares for the new geometry forms and
trace conducting paths for circuit outcomes. Existing frozen-paper digests remain
unchanged. The local-only browser fixture uses synthetic progress to test both
subject editors, mixed/practice/paper transitions, ink, the review queue, manual
repetition routing and narrow-screen controls. Software tests cannot establish
entrance-test equivalence or admission readiness.
